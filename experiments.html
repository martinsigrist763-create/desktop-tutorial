Experiments

Testing what AI can actually do

The aim is to distinguish attractive theory from capability that can be demonstrated, challenged and improved.

Societal value and business

Can AI identify how society expresses expectations of business, turn those expectations into a transparent framework, and organise public evidence about an individual company so humans can judge its societal value delivery?

Complex human decisions

Can AI take a major decision, reconstruct the information and alternatives available at the time, and produce a clear assessment that can be inspected and challenged without confusing hindsight with what was knowable then?

Scientific concepts

Can AI help turn ambiguous scientific terms into explicit sets of features and criteria so competing theories can be compared against the same object rather than different meanings of the same word?

Cross-model agreement

Where different AI models receive the same evidence and framework, does convergence between their judgements provide useful evidence that a result is robust rather than model-specific?
