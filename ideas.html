Working ideas

Short explanations of concepts that recur across the work. Each is provisional, revisable and intended to make difficult ideas easier to inspect.

Sets under load-bearing words

Words such as consciousness, intelligence, fairness and happiness can hide large differences in meaning. One approach is to treat the concept as a set: make its components, relationships and boundaries explicit, then ask what can be evidenced or measured.

AI and judgement

AI may be useful not because it is always right, but because it can take bodies of information too large for any individual to understand, structure them, expose assumptions and present them in a form humans can use to make decisions.

Human happiness

If the purpose of society is to help humans live happy lives, happiness itself cannot remain a vague word. It can be treated as a set containing dimensions such as security, health, belonging, autonomy, meaning, purpose, opportunity, hope and time.

AI-native society

AI, robotics, automation and abundant energy may reduce the amount of compulsory human labour needed to sustain society. That raises a larger question: could civilisation gradually move from being organised mainly around necessity toward being organised around greater human choice?
