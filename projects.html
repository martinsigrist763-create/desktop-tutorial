Current work

The ideas are being explored through several practical projects rather than as one finished theory.

Organisations and societal value

Exploring how organisations can make purpose, principles, values, processes and productivity more explicit, and how AI might help society understand whether businesses deliver societal as well as shareholder value.

Science and consciousness

Testing whether clearer definitions, explicit sets and stronger process disciplines can improve work on difficult scientific questions, beginning with consciousness.

AI-native society

Exploring how AI, robotics, automation and new forms of abundance could alter the relationship between work, necessity, human choice and flourishing.

Public decision-making

Exploring whether AI can make complex policy and historical decisions more transparent by organising evidence, assumptions, alternatives and counterfactuals into a form humans can inspect.
